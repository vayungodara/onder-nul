Some hours, Dutch electricity costs less than nothing. This is a warning for those hours, made two days ahead and checked against what really happened in 2026.

How far ahead does it work?

Tomorrow's prices are published every day around 13:00, so nobody needs a model for tomorrow. The question is what happens further out. The score (F1) is only high when the warnings are both right and complete.
